HEKA127 is the operating brain of RA ATUM—not a chatbot and not a replacement for consensus. It turns live evidence into transparent, reviewable operational intelligence.

Observe
Read network, validator, transaction, module, recovery and runtime evidence.
Detect
Identify anomalies, drift, stale evidence and operational risk without manufacturing certainty.
Diagnose
Trace likely root causes across runtime and source boundaries.
Shadow Test
Test candidate ideas away from the deterministic production path.
Prepare Proposal
Produce a reasoned change proposal with evidence, boundaries and rollback expectations.
Developer Review
A developer approves controlled action; no autonomous consensus or key mutation is allowed.
Verified Boundaries
  • Observe → Detect → Diagnose → Shadow Test → Proposal → Review
  • Every decision has a reason
  • Unknown remains Unknown
  • Deterministic core stays protected
