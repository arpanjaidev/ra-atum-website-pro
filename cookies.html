Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how RA ATUM uses cookies and similar technologies on its website and working-testnet interfaces.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device to improve browsing, remember preferences, and help our site function properly.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: For security, sessions, and basic interface flows.
  • Analytics Cookies: To analyze website traffic and usage (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference Cookies: To remember language, theme, and other settings.

3. How to Manage Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may affect saved preferences or testnet interface features.

4. Third-Party Cookies

We may use cookies set by service providers (e.g., Google, Vercel, GitHub Pages, analytics/CDN providers). We don’t control third-party cookies; please review their policies for details.

5. Retention

Cookie lifetimes vary. Session cookies expire when you close your browser; persistent cookies remain until they expire or are deleted.

6. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by the Effective Date above. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

7. Contact

Questions about cookies? Use the details on our Contact page.
